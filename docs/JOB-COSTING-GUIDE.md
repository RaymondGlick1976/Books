# Job Costing Guide

Working reference for what Homestead Cabinet Design (HCD) pays for materials on cabinet jobs. Figures come from supplier invoices, not estimates.

Standing rule: when pricing painted doors or cabinets, always include the hardener (Renner topcoat or primer, see section 15).

Last updated: 2026-10-19
Source: J & L Door invoices in the `Invoices J&L` folder (15 orders, Aug 2025 to Oct 2026).

---

## 1. Paint-Grade MDF Doors (J&L Door, Dalton, OH)

**Spec on the invoices:** "PAINT GRADE MDF PNL" or "PG MDF", five-piece construction with a 3/8" or 5/8" MDF panel (panel codes P-25, P-202, P-212).

**Frame wood:** the invoices do not name the frame species. Our customer quotes specify maple door frames, so this guide assumes maple. Confirm with J&L.

### Average door cost

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Doors counted | 228 (13 orders) |
| Total spend | $8,598.26 |
| **Average per door** | **$37.71** |
| Median per door | $36.03 |
| Range | $21.22 to $68.41 |
| Average door size | 387 sq in |
| Cost per sq in | $0.097 |
| Freight per door (Pack & Ship, spread across each order) | $9.12 |
| **Average landed per door** | **about $46.83** |

Figures are before J&L's 2% early-pay discount (paid within 10 days) and before the 2% late charge (after the due date). Pack & Ship is J&L's freight line.

### By size

| Door size (sq in) | Doors | Average |
|---|---|---|
| Under 250 | 30 | $25.80 |
| 250 to 400 | 104 | $33.25 |
| 400 to 600 | 82 | $45.18 |
| 600 to 800 | 12 | $55.12 |

Size drives price. Cost per sq in runs about $0.09 to $0.12, and smaller doors cost more per sq in.

### By profile (IP on the invoice)

| Profile | Doors | Average | Avg size (sq in) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rail | 62 | $41.38 | 432 |
| Davis | 31 | $39.84 | 429 |
| Bevel | 8 | $39.41 | 345 |
| Countour | 50 | $37.02 | 377 |
| Shaker | 50 | $34.61 | 347 |
| Cope | 27 | $33.37 | 345 |

Most of the gap between profiles comes from door size. Cost per sq in is $0.093 to $0.114 for every profile.

### Other paint-grade door types

- **Two-panel doors** (SRVP 2 PNL): 6 doors, average $96.59, average 1,024 sq in.
- **Glass doors** (SRVP GLASS): 8 doors, average $38.06.

### Paint-grade drawer fronts (five-piece)

- 67 pieces, average $22.58, median $20.18.
- $20.00 is J&L's minimum on small pieces, so the average sits close to that floor.

### Rules of thumb

- **Door material:** about $0.10 per sq in, with a floor near $21 per door. Check: 387 sq in x $0.097 = $37.60, which matches the $37.71 average.
- **Freight:** on the larger orders (29 to 61 pieces), Pack & Ship ran $398 to $635, about $9 to $16 per piece. A 2-piece order cost $62 ($31 per piece), so small orders are expensive to ship.
- **Price trend:** 2025 average $37.59 per door versus 2026 average $37.84. Cost per sq in went from $0.096 to $0.099, about 3% higher. Roughly flat.

| Shipping, drawer front | $6.50 | per piece | about $9 per piece (same J&L order freight) |

| Rollout, large (4" high, 18" to 36" wide) | by the piece | $80.00 | $83.44 per box (Eagle, box only; widths 19.6875" to 25.5625") |

Drawer fronts are split by height: over 7" high, or 7" and under. The J&L drawer front figures are paint-grade five-piece pieces. Their minimum charge is $20.00.

Shipping: J&L bills Pack & Ship per order, not per door. Drawer fronts ride on the same order freight, so the per-piece cost is about the same as doors (about $9 per piece across all paint-grade pieces). Working shipping rates: $12 per door and $6.50 per drawer front. On the J&L mix (228 doors, 67 drawer fronts), the blended rate comes to about $10.75 per piece.

How the size split is applied: a door is "over 27"" if its longer side is over 27 inches. Per-piece rates use that split; per-sq-ft pricing uses the actual door area.
